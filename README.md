Unity editor version: 2021.3.15f1


In what ways can speculative narratives create space 
for the imagination of alternate systems and realities?

Shebak El Hayah is an RPG-style speculative video game 
exploring bureaucracy, surveillance, temporality and 
the dynamics of power. You are tasked with getting 
a certificate of proof of life before the end of the 
day. Fellow citizens may give hints on how to hack 
the system through bartering, lament the wait or try 
to purposefully mislead you, all while time is passing 
rapidly. Will you find Ragab before he finishes his 
working day? Or will you have to pass by again 
tomorrow?

The resulting experience is meditation on systems of 
control and the gaps we can find in between. 
This was a collaborative project completed with Nour 
Bergaoui, AbulKassem Salama, as a part of the ADEF 
Malha Al Madina residency in February 2023.
